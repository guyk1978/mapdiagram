Frequently Asked Questions

What is a workflow builder tool?

A workflow builder tool is used to visually design and organize step-by-step processes using connected nodes and diagrams.

What is a workflow used for?

Workflows are used to define how tasks, decisions, and actions move from start to finish in a structured process.

Who uses workflow builder tools?

Workflow tools are used by business teams, operations managers, developers, startups, and individuals organizing processes.

Can I create workflows online for free?

Yes, MapDiagram allows you to build workflows online for free directly in your browser without installation.

What is the difference between a workflow and a flowchart?

A workflow focuses on real operational processes, while a flowchart often focuses on logic, decisions, and branching paths.
