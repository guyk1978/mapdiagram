Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the MapDiagram FAQ. Here you’ll find answers to the most common questions about our tools, features, and how to use the platform effectively.

General Questions

What is MapDiagram?

MapDiagram is a free browser-based diagram tool for creating flowcharts, mind maps, system diagrams, workflows, and more in a visual workspace.

Is MapDiagram free?

Yes, MapDiagram is completely free to use. You can start building diagrams immediately without installation.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is required. Everything runs directly in your browser.

Features

What diagrams can I create?

You can create flowcharts, mind maps, UML diagrams, decision trees, customer journey maps, data flow diagrams, and system architecture diagrams.

Can I save my work?

Yes, you can save projects locally or in your account if you are logged in.

Can I export diagrams?

Yes, diagrams can be exported depending on your workflow needs (image or structured data depending on implementation).

Use Cases

Who is MapDiagram for?

MapDiagram is used by developers, designers, founders, product managers, students, and teams who need to visualize ideas quickly.

Can I use it for business planning?

Yes, it is widely used for workflows, system design, process mapping, and business strategy planning.
