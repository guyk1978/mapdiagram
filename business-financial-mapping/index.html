When to use this workflow

Reach for this pipeline when numbers and process must stay in sync—especially where cash, approvals, and accountability cross teams.

Commercial loan or line-of-credit approval paths with treasury funding steps

CapEx requests that need finance sign-off and procurement handoffs

Entity or ownership changes that affect who approves spend

Month-end close maps tied to repayment or covenant milestones
