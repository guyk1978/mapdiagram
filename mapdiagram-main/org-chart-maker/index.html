Org Chart Maker

An org chart maker helps you visualize company structure, roles, and reporting lines. With MapDiagram, you can create clear organizational charts in minutes using a simple drag-and-drop interface.

What is an Organizational Chart?

An organizational chart (org chart) shows how teams, departments, and roles are connected inside a company. It helps clarify responsibilities and improves communication.

Why Use an Org Chart Tool?

  • Clarify reporting structure
  • Improve team alignment
  • Plan hiring and growth
  • Visualize company hierarchy

How It Works

Add nodes for roles (CEO, Manager, Team Member) and connect them vertically to represent hierarchy. You can rearrange and expand the chart easily.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an org chart maker?

An org chart maker is a visual tool used to create organizational charts that show roles, teams, and reporting structures within a company.

What is an organizational chart used for?

Organizational charts are used to visualize company hierarchy, clarify reporting lines, and improve team structure understanding.

Who uses org charts?

Org charts are used by HR teams, managers, founders, project leaders, and businesses of all sizes.

Can I create an org chart online for free?

Yes, MapDiagram allows you to create organizational charts online for free directly in your browser.

What is the difference between an org chart and a family tree?

An org chart shows workplace hierarchy and reporting structure, while a family tree shows generational relationships between family members.
