Contact

We’d love to hear from you. Whether you need help, want to report a bug, or have ideas to improve MapDiagram, you can reach our team directly.

Contact Email

For all inquiries, please email:

hello@mapdiagram.com

What You Can Contact Us About

  • Technical support and bugs
  • Feature requests for new diagram tools
  • Workflow and UX feedback
  • Collaboration or partnership inquiries

Feature Requests Guidelines

If you're requesting a new feature, try to include:

  • What problem you're trying to solve
  • How you currently work around it
  • What the ideal solution would look like

Response Time

We usually respond within a few business days. As a small team focused on product development, responses may sometimes take a bit longer — but every message is read.

Frequently Asked

Do you offer live chat support?
Not at the moment. Support is handled via email.

Can I request new diagram tools?
Yes — we actively improve MapDiagram based on user feedback.

Is MapDiagram free?
Yes, core tools are free to use in the browser.
