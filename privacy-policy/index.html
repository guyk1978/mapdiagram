Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MapDiagram collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website, visual diagram tools, workflow builder, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information (email address when you sign up)
  • Project data you create inside the editor
  • Basic usage data (such as interactions with the app)
  • Technical data (browser type, device type, performance logs)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and operate the MapDiagram editor
  • Save and sync your projects (if you are logged in)
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Fix bugs and maintain system stability

3. Project Storage

Your diagrams and workflow projects may be stored locally in your browser and/or securely in our database (if you are logged in). This allows you to access your work across devices.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share data when required to operate the service (for example, secure cloud hosting providers like Supabase).

5. Cookies and Local Storage

MapDiagram may use browser local storage or cookies to improve functionality, such as remembering your session or saving your work locally.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no online system can be 100% secure, and you use the service at your own risk.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services (such as authentication or database providers) to operate the platform. These services only process data necessary for functionality.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

hello@mapdiagram.app
