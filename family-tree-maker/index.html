What is a Family Tree?

A family tree is a diagram that shows relationships between family members across generations. It starts from a root person or couple and branches out into parents, children, grandparents, and extended relatives.

Each node in the tree represents a person, and connections show relationships such as:

  • Parent → child relationships
  • Marriage or partnership connections
  • Siblings and extended family branches

This structure helps visualize family history in a clear and organized way.

Why Use a Family Tree Maker?

Traditional genealogy research often involves documents, notes, and spreadsheets. A visual family tree simplifies everything by turning information into a structured diagram.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a family tree maker?

A family tree maker is a visual tool used to create genealogy charts and map family relationships across generations.

Can I create a family tree online for free?

Who uses family tree diagrams?

What information can I include in a family tree?
