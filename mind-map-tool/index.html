What is a Mind Map?

A mind map is a diagram used to visually organize information around a central idea. From this central concept, related ideas branch out in all directions.

Each branch represents a thought, concept, or sub-idea connected to the main topic.

This structure mimics how the human brain naturally processes ideas — through associations rather than linear lists.

Mind Maps vs Flowcharts

Mind maps and flowcharts are often confused, but they serve different purposes.

Mind maps are non-linear and focus on idea expansion, while flowcharts are structured and focus on step-by-step processes.

In MapDiagram, both tools are available, allowing you to switch between creative thinking and structured planning.
